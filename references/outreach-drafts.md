# Outreach drafts

Two drafts per confirmed move. They are drafts: a human reviews and sends. Write them as a person would write to someone they know, not as mail-merge.

## Rules for both drafts

- **Personal, not templated.** Reference the actual person, their actual move, the actual account, and (where known) something specific: the role they held, how long, a project or interaction on the record. Use the person record's notes, last interactions, and a light web check.
- **Short.** Three to five sentences. Busy senior insurance people.
- **One clear ask each.** Congrats draft: warm, no ask, or at most a soft "let us stay in touch." Intro draft: one specific ask.
- **Voice.** Plain, direct, warm. No corporate filler. No em dashes. Customer-first: lead with them, not with Axxion.
- **No fabrication.** Do not invent a relationship history, a mutual contact, or a project that is not on the record. If you do not know it, leave it out.
- **Sign-off.** Leave the sender as the person's `contact_owner` if known; otherwise a neutral placeholder the BD owner fills in. Never send; these are drafts only.

## Draft 1: Congratulations to the mover (new role)

Goal: keep the relationship warm at the new company. No hard sell.

Shape:
- Open with genuine congratulations on the specific new role and company.
- One line acknowledging the work or relationship at the old account, if the record supports it.
- A soft note that you would value staying in touch, and a light hook to their new remit if there is an honest one (only if Axxion plausibly serves their new context).
- Warm close.

Example (illustrative, always rewrite to the real person):

```
Subject: Congratulations on the move to <New Company>

Hi <First name>,

Saw the news that you have stepped into <New role> at <New Company> — congratulations, well deserved after your work leading <vacated role> at <Old insurer>.

It was good working with you on the motor-claims side these past <period>. I would love to stay in touch as you settle in; if motor claims is on your plate at <New Company>, happy to share what we are seeing in the market.

Wishing you a strong start.

<Contact owner / sender>
```

## Draft 2: Introduction request (fill the vacated seat)

Goal: keep the insurer account covered. Default recipient is the departed champion (warm handoff). If there is a stronger remaining contact at the insurer, address it to them instead and say which you chose and why.

Shape:
- Reference the move and the gap honestly.
- Ask for one introduction: to whoever now owns the vacated seat (or will).
- Make it easy: offer a one-line context they can forward.
- Low-pressure close.

Example (illustrative, always rewrite):

```
Subject: A quick introduction at <Old insurer>?

Hi <First name>,

Congratulations again on <New Company>. With you moving on, I want to make sure we keep supporting <Old insurer> well on motor claims.

Would you be open to a quick introduction to whoever is picking up <vacated role>? Even a one-line forward would help. Happy to send a short note you can pass along.

Thanks either way, and all the best in the new role.

<Contact owner / sender>
```

If addressing a remaining contact at the insurer instead, adjust: acknowledge <departed champion> has moved on, and ask who now owns <vacated role> so coverage continues.

## Where the drafts go

- Both drafts go in a **note on the person record**, under a heading with the move summary and the gap analysis, so BD has everything in one place.
- The Champion Moves list entry holds the structured routing (status, affected insurer, vacated role, new company/role, source, confidence). Keep the long-form drafts in the note, not the list fields.
- If the run is configured for Slack, the alert is one line and links nothing sensitive: "<Person> moved from <Old insurer> to <New company>. Vacated: <role>. Drafts on the record."

## Possible (unconfirmed) moves

When confidence is `Possible`, do not write outreach drafts. Write only the flag and a short note: what differs, what you checked, and the one thing a human should verify (e.g. "LinkedIn shows <New Company> but no start date; confirm before acting"). Outreach waits until a human confirms.
